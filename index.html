FAQ

Questions before ordering?

What files do you provide?

Depending on the project, final delivery can include production-ready STL, editable Rhino 3DM source files and rendered approval views.

Can you work from a sketch or photo?

Yes. Upload your reference and include dimensions, stone sizes, metal and any manufacturing instructions you have.

Can you resize an existing CAD model?

Yes. Upload the existing model and specify the new size. The decorative design and proportions can be reviewed before resizing.

How is pricing calculated?

Pricing depends on design complexity, stone setting, modeling time, revisions and deliverables. You receive a quote before the project starts.
